## Check who collected it
Open the payment to see the company name and mandate reference. Some companies collect under a trading name that differs from their brand.

## Stop future collections
Open **Payments**, then **Direct Debits**, select it and choose **Cancel**. Cancelling does not end your contract with the company.

## Getting your money back
If you did not authorise the Direct Debit, or the amount or date was wrong, contact support. An agent will review it and can raise a refund claim. Do this as soon as you notice it.

## Security
If you think someone is using your account details, tell support straight away.
