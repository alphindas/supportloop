## Common causes
- Blurred photos, glare or cut-off corners.
- An expired or damaged document.
- A document type we do not accept.
- A face that is hard to see in the selfie video.
- Details on the document that differ from your account details.

## Try again
Use good light, lay the document flat and show all four corners. Remove hats and sunglasses for the video.

## Still stuck
If verification has taken more than 2 working days, or fails repeatedly, contact support so an agent can review it.
