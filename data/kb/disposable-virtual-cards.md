## What it is
A disposable virtual card is a virtual card for one online payment. After one completed payment, its number stops working and the app creates a new number.

## How to get one
Open **Cards**, choose **Add card**, then **Disposable card**. It is free. You need to have passed identity verification first.

## Limits
You can have 1 active disposable card at a time, and the number can change up to 5 times in one day.

## When not to use it
Do not use it for subscriptions, deposits or bookings that charge the same card again, because the later charge will fail. It cannot be used at a card reader or a cash machine.

## If a payment fails
Check that the number has not already been used, that online payments are switched on under **Security settings** and that your balance covers the payment. If it still fails, contact support with the merchant name and the time.
