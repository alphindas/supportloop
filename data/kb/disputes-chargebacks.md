## When you can dispute
You can dispute a completed card payment that you did not authorise, that was charged twice, that had the wrong amount, or where goods or services were not provided as agreed.

## Time limit
Raise a dispute within 120 days of the payment. For payments you did not authorise, tell us immediately.

## How to start
Open the payment and choose **Report a problem**, then add your evidence.

## How long it takes
Most disputes are decided within 15 working days. Complex cases can take up to 45 days. A dispute does not guarantee a refund.
