## Fees on card payments
NovaPay does not charge a fee for card payments in a currency you hold enough of. If a payment needs a currency conversion, the exchange rules apply. Some merchants add their own surcharge for paying by card, especially outside the UK.

## Find out what a charge is
Open the charge in your transaction list to see its type, date and linked payment.

## Common explanations
- A temporary card verification charge, which is refunded.
- A hold by a hotel, car hire firm or fuel station.
- A cash withdrawal fee after your free allowance.
- A currency exchange fee or weekend markup.
- A subscription or renewal from a merchant.

## If you still do not recognise it
If you see a NovaPay fee that does not match our fee articles, contact support. If you think you did not authorise a charge, freeze your card and contact support.
