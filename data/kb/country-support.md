## Where you can open an account
NovaPay accounts are available to people who live in the United Kingdom or a country in the European Economic Area (EEA).

## Using your card abroad
You can use your card in other countries where your card network is accepted. Some destinations are restricted for legal or security reasons.

## Card delivery
Physical cards can only be delivered to an address in a supported country.
