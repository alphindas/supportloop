## Check first
- The merchant may trade under a different name.
- The date may be when the merchant collected the money, not when you ordered.
- It could be a subscription or a free trial that started charging.

## If it was not you
1. Freeze the card in **Cards**.
2. Choose **Report lost or stolen** to block it.
3. Contact support. A support agent must review the payment and can open a dispute.

Report it as soon as you notice it.
