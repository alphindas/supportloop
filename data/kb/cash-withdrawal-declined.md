## Common reasons
- Not enough balance to cover the amount and any fee.
- You reached the daily withdrawal limit of £500.
- Wrong or blocked PIN.
- The card is frozen, blocked, expired or not activated.
- Cash withdrawals are switched off in **Security settings**.
- Our security checks stopped the withdrawal.
- The cash machine had a fault.

## What to do
Check the reason in the app. If the app shows no attempt at all, the machine probably did not reach us. If your card keeps being declined, contact support.
