## Cards you can top up with
Visa and Mastercard debit and credit cards in your own name. American Express and prepaid gift cards are not supported.

## Currencies you can hold and exchange
GBP, EUR, USD, CHF, PLN, SEK, NOK, DKK, AUD, CAD, JPY and NZD.

## Paying in other currencies
You can pay by card in other currencies that your card network supports. NovaPay converts the amount from your balance when you pay.

## Topping up in another currency
You can top up in any currency you can hold. If your other card uses a different currency, your card provider converts it and may charge you.

## Cryptocurrency
NovaPay does not support buying, holding, exchanging or topping up with cryptocurrency.
