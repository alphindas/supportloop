## When you need this
Use this if a NovaPay card you already have does not appear in the app, for example after you removed it from your card list, or after you found a card that you had only frozen or removed.

## How to link it
Open **Cards**, choose **Add card**, then **Link a card I already have**. Enter the card number and expiry date.

## Cards that cannot be linked
You cannot link a card that was reported lost or stolen, because it is permanently blocked. You also cannot link an expired card or a card from another person's account. To add a card from another bank for topping up, see the card top-up article.
