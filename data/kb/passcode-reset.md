## Reset it
On the sign-in screen choose **Forgot passcode**. Confirm the phone number on your account, enter the one-time code we send and record a short selfie video. Then set a new passcode.

## Locked out
After several wrong attempts, sign-in is paused for a while. Use **Forgot passcode** instead of guessing.

## If the reset fails
If you cannot receive the code or the selfie check fails, contact support. An agent must confirm your identity.

## Safety
NovaPay staff will never ask for your passcode or one-time codes.
