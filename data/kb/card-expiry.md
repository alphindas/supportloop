## Expiry date
A NovaPay physical card is valid for 3 years. The expiry date is printed on the card. You can use it until the last day of that month.

## Your renewal card
We send a new card about 30 days before the old one expires. Renewal is free, including standard delivery. We send a reminder in the app before the card is posted.

## After it arrives
Activate the new card in the app. The old card stops working once the new one is active. Update the card details with any merchant that takes regular payments.
