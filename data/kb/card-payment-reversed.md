## What a reversal is
A reversed (reverted) payment was cancelled before it fully completed. The reserved money returns to your available balance and the payment may disappear from your list or show as **Reversed**.

## Common reasons
- The merchant cancelled the order.
- The merchant did not collect the money in time.
- A technical error caused a duplicate or failed attempt.

## Did the merchant still get paid?
A reversed payment does not pay the merchant. Check with them whether you need to pay again.
