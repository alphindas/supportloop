## Why we ask
Verification shows that you are the owner of the card used to top up. It protects you against fraud.

## Security check
Most card top-ups ask you to approve the payment with your card provider, for example in their app or with a one-time code.

## Verification charge
For a new card we may make a small temporary charge. Its statement description contains a 4-digit code. Find the code in your other card's statement and enter it in **Add money** then **Verify card**. The temporary charge is refunded. The description can take up to 3 working days to appear.

## Problems
If you cannot find the code after 3 working days, remove the card and add it again, or contact support.
