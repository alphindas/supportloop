## The base rate
Our rate is based on the interbank mid-market rate. This is the midpoint between the buying and selling price of a currency.

## Seeing the current rate
Open **Exchange** and choose two currencies to see the live rate before you confirm. Rates change throughout the day.

## Weekends
Currency markets are closed at weekends, so a 1% weekend markup applies from Friday 22:00 to Sunday 22:00 UK time.

## Comparing rates
Rates shown by search engines or other providers may be taken at a different time or leave out fees.
