## How conversion works
If you do not hold enough of the currency, we convert from your balance using the NovaPay rate at the time the payment or withdrawal is authorised. The final amount of a card payment may change slightly when the merchant completes it.

## Conversion by the merchant or machine
A merchant or cash machine may offer to charge you in your home currency. This uses their rate, which is often worse. Decline and choose the local currency.

## What to check
Open the transaction to see the currency, rate and fees. Compare using the rate at that time, not today's rate. Remember the weekend markup. For withdrawals, check whether you passed your free withdrawal allowance.

## Still wrong
If the rate shown does not match our rules, contact support with the receipt.
