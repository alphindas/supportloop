## Minimum age
You must be at least 18 years old to open a NovaPay account.

## Accounts for children
NovaPay does not offer accounts or cards for children, including accounts managed by a parent. Do not give your own card to a child.

## Checking your age
Your age is checked from your identity document during verification.
