## Common reasons for card top-ups
- The card details or security code were wrong.
- The security check from your card provider was not completed.
- Your card provider declined the payment.
- The card type is not supported.
- The amount is outside the top-up limits.

## What to do
Check the reason shown in the app. Confirm with your card provider that they allow the payment, then try again.

## Was money taken?
A failed top-up is not added to your NovaPay balance. If your card provider shows a pending amount, it is normally released within 7 days.
