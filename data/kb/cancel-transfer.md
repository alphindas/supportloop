## When you can cancel
You can cancel a transfer while it shows as **Scheduled** or **Pending**. Open it and choose **Cancel transfer**. If there is no cancel button, it can no longer be cancelled in the app.

## Completed transfers
A completed transfer cannot be cancelled. Ask the recipient to send the money back.

## Recall request
If you sent money to the wrong person, contact support. We can send a recall request to the receiving bank. The money is not guaranteed to be returned.

## Suspected fraud
If you think you were tricked into sending money, contact support immediately.
