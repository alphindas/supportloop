## Before you ask
Move your money out first, wait for pending payments to complete, and cancel Direct Debits and regular payments.

## How to close
Contact support and ask to close your account. A support agent must confirm it is you and close the account. You cannot close it yourself in the app.

## After closing
Your cards stop working. A closed account cannot be reopened.
