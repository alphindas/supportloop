## What pending means
The transfer has been created but has not been completed.

## Common reasons
- It is within the normal processing time.
- It was made on a weekend or public holiday.
- It is waiting for a security review.
- It is scheduled for a later date.

## What to do
Check the status and expected date in the app. Do not send the same payment again while it is pending. If it is still pending after the expected date, contact support.
