## Protect your account first
1. Freeze your card in **Cards**.
2. Choose **Report lost or stolen** if you did not make the withdrawal.
3. Change your app passcode.

## Contact support
A support agent must review every withdrawal you do not recognise. Tell us the date, time and amount, and whether you still have your card.

## Check the details
The date or location shown may be that of the machine operator. If you are sure you did not make it, report it without delay.
