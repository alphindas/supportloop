## Address, phone number and email
Open **Account**, choose **Personal details**, select the detail, enter the new value and confirm with your app passcode.

## Name
You cannot change your name yourself. Contact support with a document that shows the change, such as a marriage certificate or a new passport. A support agent must check it.

## Cards already ordered
A change of address does not redirect a card that has already been sent. Contact support if a card is on its way to your old address.
