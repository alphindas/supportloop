## Check these first
- The card is activated.
- The card is not frozen or reported lost.
- The card has not expired.
- Your balance covers the payment.
- The payment type (chip, contactless, online or cash withdrawal) is switched on under **Security settings**.
- Your daily limit has not been reached.

## Test it
Try a chip-and-PIN payment at a different shop.

## Still not working
If the card fails at several places and all checks pass, contact support. The chip may be damaged and you may need a replacement.
