## How to top up
Open **Add money**, choose **Card**, enter the amount and the card details, then approve the payment with your card provider's security check.

## Supported cards
Visa and Mastercard debit and credit cards in your own name. American Express and prepaid gift cards are not supported.

## How long it takes
Card top-ups usually arrive immediately. Some take up to 1 hour.

## Saved cards
You can save a card for faster top-ups and remove it at any time under **Add money** then **Saved cards**.
