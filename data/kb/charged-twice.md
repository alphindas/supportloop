## Check the status of both
Often one entry is **Pending** and one is **Completed**. The extra pending entry normally disappears within 7 days.

## Two completed payments
First ask the merchant for a refund. Check that they are not two separate purchases.

## Open a dispute
If both payments are completed and the merchant will not help, open the extra payment and choose **Report a problem**, or contact support. Include any receipt.
