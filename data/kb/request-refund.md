## Ask the merchant first
For a purchase you made, the merchant decides on refunds. Contact them with your order details.

## If the merchant will not help
You may be able to dispute the payment, for example if goods did not arrive, were not as described or you were charged the wrong amount.

## Transfers
A bank transfer cannot be refunded by NovaPay once completed. See the article about cancelling a transfer.

## Evidence to keep
Keep receipts, order confirmations and your messages with the merchant.
