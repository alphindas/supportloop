## Why it is pending
The cash machine operator has not yet sent the final record of your withdrawal.

## How long it takes
Most pending withdrawals complete or disappear within 7 days.

## If you got no cash
If the machine gave you no money but the amount is pending, wait up to 7 days for it to be released. If it completes instead, contact support to dispute it.
