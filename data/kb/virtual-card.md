## Creating a virtual card
Open **Cards**, choose **Add card**, then **Virtual card**. It is free and ready immediately. You need to have passed identity verification first.

## Using it
View the card number, expiry date and security code in the app. Use them to pay online. You can have up to 3 active virtual cards. A virtual card on its own cannot be inserted into or tapped on a card reader.

## If it is not working
Check that the card is not frozen, that online payments are switched on under **Security settings**, that your balance covers the payment and that you copied the current card details. Some merchants, such as some car hire firms and hotels, ask for a physical card. If a new virtual card also fails, contact support with the merchant name and the time of the attempt.

## Removing it
Select the virtual card and choose **Delete card**. A deleted virtual card cannot be restored, so move any subscriptions to another card first.
