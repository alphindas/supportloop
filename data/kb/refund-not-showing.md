## How long refunds take
After a merchant sends a refund, it usually takes 5 to 10 working days to appear.

## Where to look
Check your transaction list for a separate incoming entry. If the original payment was still pending, it may simply disappear instead of showing a refund.

## Ask the merchant
After 10 working days, ask the merchant for proof of the refund, including the date, amount and refund reference.

## Contact support
Send that proof to support so an agent can trace the refund.
