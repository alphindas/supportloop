## How to do it
1. Open **Add money** and choose **Bank transfer**.
2. Copy the account details shown for the currency you are sending.
3. Send the money from your other bank, using any reference shown in the app.

## How long it takes
- UK bank transfers: usually the same day, at most 1 working day.
- SEPA transfers in euros: 1 working day.
- International (SWIFT) transfers: 3 to 5 working days.

## Cost
NovaPay does not charge for receiving a UK or SEPA bank transfer. Your other bank may charge you.
