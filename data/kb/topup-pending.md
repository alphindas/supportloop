## What pending means
The top-up has started but the money has not been confirmed yet. You cannot spend it until it completes.

## Normal waiting times
- Card top-up: usually immediate, up to 1 hour.
- Cash deposit: usually 30 minutes, up to 1 working day.
- UK bank transfer: up to 1 working day.
- International bank transfer: 3 to 5 working days.

## What to do
Do not repeat the top-up while it is pending, or you may pay twice. If it is still pending after the times above, contact support.
