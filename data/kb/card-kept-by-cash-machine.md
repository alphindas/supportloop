## Freeze the card
Open **Cards**, select the card and choose **Freeze**. This stops new payments straight away.

## Do not wait at the machine for help from strangers
Never share your PIN with anyone who offers to help. If the machine is inside a bank or shop, tell the staff, and note the place and time.

## Get a new card
A machine that keeps a card may not give it back. Choose **Report lost or stolen** to block the card for good and order a replacement. If you think someone tampered with the machine, contact support straight away.
