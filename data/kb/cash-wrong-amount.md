## What to do
1. Count the cash and keep any receipt.
2. Note the time, the location and the machine operator.
3. Contact support and tell us the amount you asked for and the amount you got.

## What happens next
An agent opens a dispute. We ask the machine operator to check its records. This usually takes up to 15 working days, and complex cases can take up to 45 days.

## Tip
The payment may first show as pending. Report the problem anyway so we can record it.
