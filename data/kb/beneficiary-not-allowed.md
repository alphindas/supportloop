## What the message means
**Beneficiary not allowed** means that NovaPay will not send a payment to that recipient.

## Possible reasons
- The destination country or currency is not supported.
- The receiving account type does not accept this kind of transfer.
- The recipient details failed a required check.
- Security or legal rules block the payment.

## What you can do
Check the recipient's details and currency. If they are correct, contact support. For legal and security reasons we cannot always explain the exact cause.
