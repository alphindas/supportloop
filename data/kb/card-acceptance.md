## Where it works
You can use your card at merchants that accept your card's network. This includes most shops and websites, in the UK and abroad.

## Visa or Mastercard
NovaPay issues Visa and Mastercard debit cards. When both are available for your country, you can choose one when you order the card. The logo is printed on your physical card and shown in the app. To change network you need a replacement card, which costs £5.

## Where it may be refused
- Unattended machines, such as some pay-at-pump fuel stations and toll booths.
- Some car hire firms, which may ask for a credit card.
- Merchants that do not accept debit cards.

## Temporary holds
Hotels, car hire firms and fuel stations may hold an amount larger than the final bill. The difference is released later.
