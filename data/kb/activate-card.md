## How to activate
1. Open **Cards** in the app.
2. Select the new card and choose **Activate**.
3. Enter the last 4 digits printed on the card.

The card is active straight away.

## First use
Make your first payment by inserting the card and entering your PIN. Contactless starts working after that.

## If activation fails
Check that you are activating the newest card and that your app is up to date. If it still fails, contact support.
