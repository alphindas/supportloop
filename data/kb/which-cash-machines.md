## Where you can withdraw
You can use your physical card at cash machines that show the logo of your card's network, Visa or Mastercard, in the UK and abroad. NovaPay has no cash machines of its own.

## What you need
An activated physical card, with cash withdrawals switched on under **Security settings**. A virtual card cannot be used at a cash machine.

## Charges and limits
Your withdrawal allowance, fees and daily limit apply at every machine. The operator may add its own charge. See the cash withdrawals article for the amounts.
