## Your account details
Open **Account** then **Account details**. Choose a currency to see the details to share with the sender, such as a sort code and account number or an IBAN.

## From NovaPay users
Another NovaPay user can send money to you using your NovaPay username.

## Salary and regular income
You can give your account details to an employer or another payer. The money arrives like any other bank transfer.

## Other currencies
If you receive a currency for which you have no account details, it may be converted on the way by the sending bank.
