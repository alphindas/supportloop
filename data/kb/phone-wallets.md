## Paying with your phone
NovaPay cards cannot currently be added to Apple Pay, Google Pay or other phone wallets.

## Adding money
You cannot add money to NovaPay through Apple Pay or Google Pay. Use a debit or credit card, a bank transfer or a cash deposit.
