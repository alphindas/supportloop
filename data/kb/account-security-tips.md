## Protect your sign-in
Use a passcode you do not use elsewhere and switch on biometric unlock if your device supports it.

## Spot scams
NovaPay will never ask you to move money to a "safe account", share a one-time code, or tell us your passcode or PIN. End any call or chat that asks for these.

## Card controls
In **Security settings** you can switch online payments, contactless and cash withdrawals on or off, and freeze a card.

## If something is wrong
Freeze your card and contact support immediately.
