## Available methods
- By debit or credit card issued by another provider.
- By bank transfer to your NovaPay account details.
- By cash at a NovaPay Cash Point partner shop.

Cheques are not accepted.

## Choosing a method
Card top-ups are usually the fastest. A bank transfer is a good choice for larger amounts. Each method has its own fees and limits.
