## Usual times
- To another NovaPay user: immediate.
- To a UK bank account: usually within 2 hours, at most 1 working day.
- SEPA: 1 working day.
- International (SWIFT): 1 to 5 working days.

## What can add time
Weekends, public holidays, the receiving bank's own processing and security reviews can all delay a transfer.

## Tracking
Open the transfer in your transaction list to see its status and expected arrival date.
