## Where is my PIN?
Your PIN is not sent in the post. Open **Cards**, select your physical card and choose **View PIN**. You will be asked for your app passcode.

## Changing your PIN
Open **Cards**, select the card, choose **PIN** and then **Change PIN**. Enter a new 4-digit PIN twice. The change works for your next chip-and-PIN payment. Insert the card for that payment so the chip updates.

## Unblocking a blocked PIN
Your PIN is blocked after 3 wrong attempts in a row. Open **Cards**, select the card and choose **Unblock PIN**. Confirm with your app passcode, then check the correct number with **View PIN**. If the app does not offer **Unblock PIN** or the card is still refused, contact support.

## Keeping it safe
Never share your PIN. NovaPay staff will never ask for it.
