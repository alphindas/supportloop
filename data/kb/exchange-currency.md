## How to exchange
Open **Exchange**, choose the currency you have and the currency you want, enter an amount, check the rate and fee, then confirm.

## Timing
The exchange happens immediately and the new balance is ready to use.

## Supported currencies
You can exchange between GBP, EUR, USD, CHF, PLN, SEK, NOK, DKK, AUD, CAD, JPY and NZD.

## Reversing an exchange
A completed exchange cannot be cancelled. You can exchange the money back at the rate available at that time.
