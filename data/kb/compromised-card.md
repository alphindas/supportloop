## Act immediately
1. Freeze the card in **Cards**.
2. Choose **Report lost or stolen** to block it permanently.
3. Contact support so an agent can check recent activity.

## Warning signs
- Payments you do not recognise.
- You entered your card details on a website you now think was fake.
- Someone asked you for your card number, security code or a one-time code.

## What happens next
We issue a new card number. A support agent reviews any payments you did not make. This kind of case is always handled by a person.
