## Types of transfer
- To another NovaPay user.
- To a UK bank account.
- To a euro account by SEPA.
- To other international accounts by SWIFT.

## How to send
Open **Payments**, choose or add a recipient, enter the amount and reference, check the details and confirm with your passcode.

## Check details carefully
A transfer sent to the wrong account may not be recoverable. Check the name and account details before you confirm.
