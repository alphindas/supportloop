## Delivery estimates
- UK standard delivery: 5 to 7 working days.
- UK express delivery: 2 to 3 working days.
- Delivery to supported countries outside the UK: 7 to 14 working days. Express delivery is not available there.

Weekends and public holidays are not working days.

## Tracking your card
Open **Cards** and select the card on order to see its status and estimated arrival date. Express orders also show a courier tracking number.

## My card has not arrived
If your card has not arrived by the latest estimated date, open the card in the app and choose **Report not delivered**. We cancel the missing card and send a new one at no cost. A support agent must check the order before the replacement is sent.
