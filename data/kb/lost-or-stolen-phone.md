## Protect your account
Contact support from another device or by asking someone you trust to help you reach us. Support can sign your account out of the lost phone and block access until you are back.

## What else to do
Use your phone maker's service to lock or erase the phone, and ask your mobile provider to block the SIM.

## Can someone use the app?
The app asks for your passcode or biometric unlock. If someone else may know your passcode, tell support at once.

## On a new phone
Install the app, sign in and verify your identity if asked. Then change your passcode and check recent activity.
