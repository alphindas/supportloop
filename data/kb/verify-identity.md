## Why we need it
Financial services rules require us to confirm who our customers are. Verification also protects your account against fraud and money laundering. It cannot be skipped. You need it before you can use your account fully, including getting cards and sending money. We use your documents for identity and legal checks, as described in the privacy notice in the app.

## What you need
One of these, valid and in your own name: a passport, a driving licence or a national identity card. You also record a short selfie video.

## Steps
1. Open **Account** then **Verify identity**.
2. Choose your document and take clear photos of it.
3. Record the selfie video and submit.

## How long it takes
Most checks finish within minutes. Some take up to 2 working days. We notify you in the app.
