## First checks
1. Open the transfer and confirm its status is **Completed**.
2. Check that the expected arrival time has passed.
3. Check the recipient's account details.

## Proof of payment
Open the transfer and choose **Download confirmation**. The recipient can give it to their bank to help find the money.

## Wrong details
If the details were wrong, contact support quickly. We can ask the receiving bank to return the money, but we cannot guarantee it.

## Still missing
If the details were right and the time has passed, contact support so an agent can trace it.
