## See the reason
Open the declined payment in your transaction list. The app shows a reason when one is available.

## Common reasons
- Not enough money in your balance.
- Wrong PIN, expiry date or security code.
- The card is frozen, blocked or expired.
- The payment is above your daily card payment limit of £5,000.
- Our security checks stopped an unusual payment.
- The merchant's own system rejected it.

## What to do
Fix the cause shown in the app and try again. If no reason is shown, or the same payment is declined again, contact support. A declined payment does not take money from your account. If an amount shows as pending, it is released automatically.
