## What it is
We sometimes ask where your money came from. This is a legal check. It can happen after large or unusual payments, such as bank transfers over £10,000.

## Documents we may accept
- Recent payslips.
- Bank statements showing the money.
- A sale agreement, for example for a property or a car.
- Documents for an inheritance or a gift.

## How to send them
Open the request in **Account** then **Documents** and upload clear copies.

## Timing
Reviews usually take up to 3 working days. Some payments may be limited until the review ends.
