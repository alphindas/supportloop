## What happened
A reversed (or reverted) top-up is one that was cancelled after it first appeared. The amount is removed from your balance and returned to where it came from.

## Possible reasons
- Your card provider cancelled or did not settle the payment.
- Our security checks could not confirm the payment.
- The card or bank account was not in your own name.
- A required verification step was not completed.

## When you get the money back
Money returned to a card or bank account usually arrives within 5 to 10 working days. If it has not arrived by then, contact support.
