## Check the timing
UK transfers can take up to 1 working day, SEPA transfers 1 working day and SWIFT transfers 3 to 5 working days.

## Check the details
Ask the sender to confirm the account details, currency and reference they used.

## Balance not updated
Pull down to refresh the app and check the balance for the correct currency.

## Contact support
If the expected time has passed, contact support with the sender's proof of payment showing the date, amount, reference and account details used. An agent will trace the payment.
