## Wait first
Cash deposits usually appear within 30 minutes. In some cases they take up to 1 working day.

## Check the receipt
Confirm that the shop gave you a receipt and that the amount on it is correct.

## Contact support
If the money is still missing after 1 working day, contact support with a photo of the receipt, the shop name and the time of the deposit. An agent will trace it.

## If you sent a cheque
NovaPay does not accept cheques, so a cheque will not be added to your balance. Contact support if you posted one to us.
