## What pending means
The merchant has reserved the money but has not collected it yet. The amount is taken from your available balance.

## How long it takes
Most pending payments complete or are released within 7 days. Hotels and car hire firms can take up to 30 days.

## Cancelled orders
If a merchant cancelled the order, ask them to release the payment. If they do not, the hold ends automatically within the times above.

## Disputing
A payment cannot be disputed while it is still pending. If it completes and is wrong, contact support.
