## Common reasons
- Not enough money to cover the amount and any fee.
- Wrong or incomplete recipient details.
- The receiving bank rejected the payment.
- Our security checks stopped the transfer.
- Your identity or source of funds check is not complete.
- The recipient or destination is not allowed.

## Where your money is
If a transfer fails after the money left your balance, it is returned to your account. A return from another bank can take up to 5 working days.

## Next step
Check the reason in the app, correct it and try again. If no reason is shown, contact support.
